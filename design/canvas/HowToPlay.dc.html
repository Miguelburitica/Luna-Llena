How to play

A few villagers are secretly wolves. Find them before they eat everyone.

Night
Everyone closes their eyes. Wolves choose a victim. Special roles use their powers in secret.
Dawn
The narrator reads what happened. Someone may not wake up.
Day
Talk on your call and in chat. Accuse, defend, lie a little.
Vote
Tap a suspect and lock your vote. Most votes is out.
Execution
The chosen one leaves the village (comically). Then night falls again.
Village wins when every wolf is gone
Wolves win when they equal the village
